InternNova Data Analytics Project

Project Title

AdventureWorks Sales Data Analysis

Project Overview

This project is done as part of the InternNova internship. The main aim is to study sales data, clean it, find useful patterns and make a dashboard from it.

Problem Statement

The business has a large amount of sales data. It is difficult to understand sales growth, product categories, regions and profit just by looking at raw data. So the data is analysed to get clear business insights.

Dataset Description

The dataset is AdventureWorks Sales data. It contains sales, product, customer, reseller, date and sales territory information.

Main tables used:

Sales Order data

Sales data

Product data

Customer data

Reseller data

Date data

Sales Territory data

Tools Used

Data Cleaning Process

Checked rows, columns, missing values and duplicates.

Checked invalid or non-positive sales values.

Checked key matching between related tables.

Product Color missing values were replaced with Unknown.

Missing ShipDateKey values were kept as unavailable instead of making fake dates.

Date fields were checked and standardized.

EDA

The analysis was done for sales amount, product cost, profit, order quantity and unit price.

Some main findings:

FY2020 had the highest sales and profit.

Bikes are the main sales category.

Accessories have a much better profit margin.

Sales and total product cost have a strong positive relationship.

Some regions have high sales but comparatively lower margins.

Outliers were checked using IQR method.

Key Insights

Sales are increasing year by year and FY2020 is the strongest.

Bikes are responsible for most of the sales.

Accessories have a high profit margin even though their sales are lower.

Profit performance is different across regions.

Higher sales also come with higher product costs.

Business Recommendations

Keep enough stock for high-selling products.

Promote accessories with bikes using bundles and cross-selling.

Check pricing, discounts and shipping cost in low-margin regions.

Focus on profit margin, not only total sales.

Give more attention to regions and products performing well.

Conclusion

The analysis shows that the business is growing, but sales and profit should be looked at together. Bikes drive most revenue, while accessories give good margins. The dashboard makes these patterns easier to understand and can help in taking better business decisions.
